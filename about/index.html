About us

Why LetterToolkit exists

LetterToolkit is an independent collection of free browser-based tools for counting, analyzing, cleaning and formatting text, with additional word-finding tools. It was built around a simple idea: common text tasks should be quick to solve without creating an account or fighting through a complicated interface.

What the site does

The main toolkit includes word, character, letter, sentence, paragraph and line counters; reading and speaking time calculators; word-frequency analysis; case conversion; text cleaning; line cleanup; list utilities; and find-and-replace. A secondary word-tools area includes Word Finder, Word Unscrambler, Anagram Solver, rhyme tools and searchable word lists.

How searches work

Most word searches are processed in your browser after the shared dictionary has loaded. This makes repeated searches fast and means the letters and patterns you type do not need to be sent to a LetterToolkit search server. Some normal web infrastructure may still receive standard request information when pages and assets are loaded.

Our dictionary

LetterToolkit uses a large English word dataset and provides attribution in the project licensing information. A large dictionary is useful for discovery, but no single list matches every game, school, publisher or regional spelling standard. Uncommon results should therefore be checked against the official dictionary or rules for the activity you are doing.

Editorial approach

Our guides are written to explain strategies and concepts rather than merely repeat lists of keywords. We aim to distinguish between a tool result and an official ruling, give practical examples, and update pages when a feature or explanation becomes inaccurate.

For the rules behind our counters, estimates and word tools, see How LetterToolkit Works. Our Editorial and Tool Quality Policy explains testing, review, automation, corrections and how we separate useful tools from automatically generated reference inventory.

Who is responsible for the site?

LetterToolkit is maintained as an independent web-tool project. The site itself is responsible for the published tools and explanations rather than presenting anonymous third-party submissions as editorial content. Corrections are reviewed against the documented tool behavior before pages are updated.

Privacy and sustainability

The core tools are free. LetterToolkit may use advertising to support hosting and continued development. Advertising and analytics, when enabled, are described in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell access to puzzle answers and we do not require an account for the core tools.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a broken tool, misleading explanation, questionable word or accessibility problem, please contact us. Useful reports include the page address, the input you used and what you expected to happen.
