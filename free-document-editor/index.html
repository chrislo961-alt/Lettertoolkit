Documents in your browser

A free document editor for everyday work

Open LetterToolkit Writer and begin with a blank page or a practical template. The editor handles the common jobs people expect from a lightweight document tool while keeping the interface easy to understand.

Open Writer – free
01

Common formatting included

Use headings, bold, italic, underline, colors, lists, links and alignment. Page size, margins, language and word goals are available without hiding the writing surface behind a complex ribbon.

02

Open and download useful formats

Import DOCX, plain text, HTML or Markdown. Export DOCX, TXT, HTML or Markdown, or use the browser print dialog to save a PDF.

03

Know the limits

Advanced Word layouts, macros, comments, tracked changes, charts and complex columns may be simplified. Use a full office suite when exact layout fidelity is essential.

Before you begin

No account is required. Documents are stored in this browser, so clearing site data can remove them. Download important work as a backup. DOCX support covers common content and may simplify advanced layouts.

How to use LetterToolkit Writer

  1. Open Writer and choose a blank document or template.
  2. Write directly or open a DOCX, TXT, HTML or Markdown file.
  3. Format and review the document while local autosave protects the draft.
  4. Download DOCX, TXT, HTML or Markdown, or print to PDF.
