How to write a strong job application

Match the job, not a generic template

Use the job advertisement as the reference point. Highlight experience and skills that are actually relevant to the role.

Keep every claim factual

A strong application does not need invented achievements. Clear examples and accurate experience are more credible.

Be specific

Replace broad phrases with concrete responsibilities, tools, environments or outcomes you can genuinely support.

Review before sending

Use the AI review, then read the final text yourself. The application should still sound like you.

Frequently asked questions

How does the AI application builder work?

Add your CV or background, paste the job advertisement and choose a tone. LetterToolkit generates a draft based on the information you provide.

Can I use an existing CV?

Yes. Upload or paste your existing CV information and use it as the factual basis for the application.

Which languages are supported?

The current version supports English, Norwegian and Swedish.

Should I send the AI text without editing it?

No. Always review the final application for factual accuracy, tone and personal fit before sending it to an employer.
