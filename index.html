FAQ

About LetterToolkit

What is LetterToolkit?

LetterToolkit is a free collection of browser-based tools for counting, analyzing and formatting text, plus a smaller set of word and letter utilities.

Is my text uploaded?

The homepage counter performs its calculations in your browser. Standard page-loading requests still occur when the website and its assets are loaded.

Can I still use the word-game tools?

Yes. Word Finder, Word Unscrambler, Anagram Solver, word lists and related tools remain available under the Word tools area.
