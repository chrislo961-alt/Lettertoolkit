About the results

Useful candidates, not official rulings

Where do the words come from?

LetterToolkit searches a large English word dataset. Because publishers and games use different dictionaries, uncommon results should be checked against the rules that apply to your activity.

Are searches sent to a server?

Most core word searches filter the downloaded dictionary directly in your browser. Standard hosting requests still occur when pages and assets are loaded.

Who is LetterToolkit for?

The site is intended for puzzle solving, vocabulary exploration, classroom practice and everyday writing. It is an independent resource and is not an official dictionary for third-party games.
