How to use the Word Unscrambler

  1. Enter your letters. Type between 2 and 15 letters.
  2. Choose filters. Set minimum or maximum word length when needed.
  3. Find words. Results are shown with the longest matches first.

What is a word unscrambler?

A word unscrambler checks which dictionary words can be formed from the letters you provide. It never uses a letter more times than it appears in your input.

Word game tips

Start with longer words, then inspect shorter combinations. Common endings such as -er, -ing, and -ed can help you spot additional possibilities.
