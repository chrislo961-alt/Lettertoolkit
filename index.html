    FAQ

    Common questions

    Are these official game dictionaries?

    No. Word lists differ between games. Verify unusual words against the rules of your game.

    What does a question mark mean?

    In the unscrambler it is a wildcard tile. In pattern searches it represents an unknown position.

    Are searches stored?

    No. Searches run in your browser and are not sent to a server.
