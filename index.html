    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a word unscrambler?

    It is a tool that finds words that can be built from a group of letters. It is useful for anagrams, spelling practice, and many letter-based games.

    Can I use blank tiles?

    Yes. Enter a question mark for each wildcard. A wildcard may replace any one missing letter.

    Does this tool send my letters anywhere?

    No. After the dictionary loads, searches run entirely in your browser.

    Are all results valid in every word game?

    No single word list matches every game. Tournament and app dictionaries differ, so verify unusual words against the rules for your game.
