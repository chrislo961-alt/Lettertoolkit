FAQ

Common questions

Is LetterToolkit free?

Yes. The current tools are free to use without an account.

Are searches stored?

No. Searches run in your browser and are not sent to a search server.

Are all words valid in every game?

No. Game dictionaries differ, so verify unusual results against the rules of your game.
