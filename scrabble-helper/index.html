    Are board bonuses included?

    No. Scores use standard English Scrabble tile values and exclude double-letter, triple-letter, double-word, triple-word, and bingo bonuses.

    Are all results tournament legal?

    Word lists vary by country and ruleset. Verify unusual words against the official dictionary used in your game.

    How do blank tiles work?

    Enter ? for each blank. The finder treats it as any letter, while the displayed score is the word's normal tile value rather than a board-adjusted blank score.
