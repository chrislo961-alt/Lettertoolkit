    How to use it

    Turn rack letters into scoring options

    1

    Enter the rack exactly

    Type each available tile once. Use ? for each blank tile.

    2

    Match the board

    Use starts, contains, ends, required and excluded letters to remove plays that cannot fit your position. Repeated required letters are counted separately.

    3

    Apply board bonuses yourself

    The displayed score is a rack-based tile score only. Add letter multipliers, word multipliers and any bingo bonus from the actual board.

    Are board bonuses included?

    No. Scores exclude double/triple letter, double/triple word and bingo bonuses.

    Are all results tournament legal?

    Not necessarily. LetterToolkit uses a broad English word dataset rather than claiming an official game dictionary. Verify unusual words against the ruleset used in your game.

    How do blank tiles affect the score?

    Enter ? for each blank. The blank may represent any needed letter, but that represented letter contributes 0 points to the displayed rack score.
