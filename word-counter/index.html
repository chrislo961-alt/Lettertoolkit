Word count rules

What counts as a word?

LetterToolkit counts sequences of letters or numbers as words and keeps common apostrophes and hyphens inside a word. Multiple spaces, tabs and line breaks do not create extra words. Different editors can use slightly different rules, so a formal submission system may differ by a small amount for unusual punctuation.

Does this count letters too?

Yes. The character totals show the full character count and a second count with whitespace removed.

What is the difference between words and characters?

A word is a sequence such as toolkit. Characters count each letter, number, punctuation mark and, in the total-character figure, spaces too.

Is the text saved or uploaded?

The counter runs in your browser. LetterToolkit does not need to send the text to a word-count service to calculate these totals.
