    Why full feedback matters

    Duplicate letters need position-aware rules

    A simple list of green, yellow and gray letters loses information. Yellow means a letter is present but not in the position where you tried it, and a gray duplicate can mean the answer contains fewer copies of that letter than your guess.

    This helper stores the entire guess and the status of each tile. It then calculates the feedback that every candidate would have produced and keeps only candidates that match your real game feedback exactly.

    Example with repeated letters

    If a guess contains two copies of E but only one receives positive feedback, the gray E does not necessarily mean E is absent. It can mean the answer contains exactly one E. Full guess feedback handles that distinction automatically.

    Candidate lists are not official answer lists

    LetterToolkit searches a broad English dictionary. It is not connected to Wordle or The New York Times, and results are not claims about an official daily answer set. Use the shortlist to reason about likely candidates and verify uncommon words when needed.

    Does this handle duplicate letters?

    Yes. The solver reproduces green matches first, then assigns yellow matches from the remaining letter counts, which mirrors the important duplicate-letter behavior.

    Can I save my current clues?

    Yes. Use Copy search link to create a URL containing up to six completed guess rows and tile states.

    Is this affiliated with Wordle?

    No. LetterToolkit is independent and is not affiliated with The New York Times or Wordle.
