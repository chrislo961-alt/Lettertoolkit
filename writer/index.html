What to expect

Writer is designed for common documents, not pixel-perfect reproduction of complex Word files. Columns, tracked changes, comments, embedded charts and advanced page layouts may be simplified.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Microsoft Word?

No. LetterToolkit Writer is an independent, lightweight word processor and is not affiliated with Microsoft.

Where are my documents saved?

They are stored in this browser using local device storage. Clearing site data can remove them, so download important work as DOCX or another backup format.

Can I edit a DOCX file?

Yes. Writer extracts common paragraphs, headings, lists and inline formatting. Complex layouts may not look identical to the original.

How do I install it?

Chrome and Edge may show an Install Writer button. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
