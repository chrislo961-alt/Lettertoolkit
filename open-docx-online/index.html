Browser-based DOCX access

Open a DOCX file online without uploading it

Choose a DOCX file from your device and LetterToolkit Writer extracts common document content locally in the browser. The imported file becomes a new local document, leaving the original untouched.

Open Writer – free
01

What is preserved

The importer handles ordinary paragraphs, headings, lists and common inline formatting such as bold, italic, underline and strikethrough.

02

What may be simplified

Tables, headers, footers, tracked changes, comments, embedded media, charts, columns and exact pagination are not guaranteed to survive import.

03

Download a fresh copy

After editing, export a standards-based DOCX package. For legally or visually sensitive documents, compare the downloaded result with the original in a full office application.

Before you begin

No account is required. Documents are stored in this browser, so clearing site data can remove them. Download important work as a backup. DOCX support covers common content and may simplify advanced layouts.

How to use LetterToolkit Writer

  1. Open Writer and choose a blank document or template.
  2. Write directly or open a DOCX, TXT, HTML or Markdown file.
  3. Format and review the document while local autosave protects the draft.
  4. Download DOCX, TXT, HTML or Markdown, or print to PDF.
