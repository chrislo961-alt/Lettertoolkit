How LetterToolkit approaches content

Original explanations

Guides are written to explain methods and trade-offs, not to pad pages with repeated keywords.

Tools are not authority

Word lists differ between publishers and games. We call out limits and encourage checking official rules for edge cases.

Useful over numerous

We prefer a smaller set of pages with clear purpose over thousands of near-duplicate pages created only for search traffic.
