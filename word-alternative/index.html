A lighter writing tool

A simple alternative to Word for quick documents

If you only need a clean place to write, format and download a document, a full office subscription can be more than the task requires. LetterToolkit Writer focuses on fast access and local privacy.

Open Writer – free
01

Where Writer fits

It works well for letters, essays, meeting notes, CV drafts, cover letters and uncomplicated reports. You can start immediately and export the result as DOCX.

02

Where Word remains stronger

Microsoft Word offers collaboration, review workflows, advanced references, mail merge, macros and precise layout control. Writer does not pretend to replace those specialist features.

03

Independent and private

LetterToolkit Writer is not affiliated with Microsoft. Documents are stored in the current browser rather than a LetterToolkit cloud account.

Before you begin

No account is required. Documents are stored in this browser, so clearing site data can remove them. Download important work as a backup. DOCX support covers common content and may simplify advanced layouts.

How to use LetterToolkit Writer

  1. Open Writer and choose a blank document or template.
  2. Write directly or open a DOCX, TXT, HTML or Markdown file.
  3. Format and review the document while local autosave protects the draft.
  4. Download DOCX, TXT, HTML or Markdown, or print to PDF.
