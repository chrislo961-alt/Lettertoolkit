Interview questions: prepare answers that stay true to your experience

Interview preparation works best when you practice likely questions using examples you can actually explain in detail.

Prepare for your interview → Application Builder →

Tell me about yourself

Prepare a concise career summary that connects your background to the role you are interviewing for.

Why do you want this job?

Combine a genuine reason for your interest with something specific about the responsibilities or company.

Tell me about a difficult situation

Use a real example. Explain the situation, your responsibility, what you did and what happened.

What are your strengths?

Choose strengths that matter for the role and support them with a real example.

Why should we hire you?

Summarize the strongest overlap between the employer's needs and your actual background.

Frequently asked questions

Should interview answers be memorized?

Practice the structure and examples rather than memorizing a script word for word.

What is the STAR method?

STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action and Result. It is a useful structure for explaining behavioral examples clearly.
