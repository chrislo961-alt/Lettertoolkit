    Does spelling always equal pronunciation?

    No. This tool finds spelling-based rhymes, so some results may not be perfect sound rhymes.

    What match length should I use?

    Start with three letters. Use two for more ideas or four and five for tighter matches.

    Are searches private?

    Yes. The search runs locally in your browser after the shared dictionary loads.
