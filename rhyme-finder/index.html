    How ranking works

    Strong, Medium and Broad spelling rhymes

    This tool compares written endings rather than pronunciation. A result matching five or more ending letters is labeled Strong, four letters is Medium, and shorter qualifying matches are Broad.

    1

    Start with three letters

    Three ending letters usually gives a useful balance between variety and relevance.

    2

    Tighten when needed

    Raise the minimum match to four or five letters when the result list is too broad.

    3

    Judge by sound yourself

    English spelling and pronunciation do not always agree, so verify promising results by saying them aloud.

    Does spelling always equal pronunciation?

    No. This tool ranks spelling-based ending matches, so some results may not be perfect sound rhymes.

    What do Strong, Medium and Broad mean?

    They describe suffix-match strength, not phonetic rhyme quality: five or more matching ending letters is Strong, four is Medium, and shorter qualifying matches are Broad.

    Are searches private?

    Yes. The search runs locally in your browser after the shared dictionary loads.
